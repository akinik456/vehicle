LynraFleet

Kısa Açıklama
Track company vehicles in real time using the driver's Android phone.

Tam Açıklama
LynraFleet turns the driver's Android phone into a vehicle tracking device. Track your company vehicles in real time from the LynraFleet mobile app or web panel.

Monitor vehicle locations, speed, GPS status, battery level, route history, and vehicle status without installing dedicated GPS tracking hardware.

Create a private fleet, add your vehicles, and monitor your fleet from anywhere.

Features

• Live vehicle tracking
• Route history
• Speed information
• Odometer and trip distance
• Vehicle status
• GPS and battery monitoring
• Smart alerts
• Fleet management
• Web dashboard access
• Fast and easy setup

LynraFleet works together with the LynraFleet Vehicle app installed on the Android phone used in the vehicle. Vehicle location is shared only with authorized members of the same fleet.

Your fleet data is private and shared only within your organization.
